# AVERY-Documentation
Avery is a humanoid animatronic designed to sing and gesture to an audience. It has digital eyes that look around and a mechancial jaw that swings to the lyrics of music. 
This project began in the summer of 2021 when I was about sixteen years old. Avery was not initally intended to be my senior engineering project, but I adopted it as such during my junior year of high school after his prototype was built.
